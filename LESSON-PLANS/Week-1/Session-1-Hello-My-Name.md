## 📍 DETAILED LESSON STEPS

### **[0-3 MIN] GREETING AT DOOR**

**What to do:**
- Stand at classroom door
- Greet EACH learner individually as they enter
- Say: "Hello! I'm [Your Name]. What's your name?"
- Help them respond
- NOTE reactions and confidence levels

**Teacher Language:**
"Hello! Welcome! I'm Teacher [Name]. What's your name?"

**Why:** Creates warm, welcoming environment. Reduces anxiety. Begins communication immediately.

---

### **[3-8 MIN] WARM-UP: ICE BREAKER**

**Activity: Two Truths, One Lie (Modified)**

**Instructions:**
1. Say: "Listen to me. Three things about me:"
   - "I'm a teacher"
   - "I'm from Canada"
   - "I speak French"
2. Ask: "Which one is a lie? Show with fingers!"
3. Learners vote (1, 2, or 3 fingers)
4. Reveal: "Yes! I'm NOT from Canada. I'm from [actual country]!"
5. Choral repetition: "I'm from [country]!" (3x)

**Teacher Language:**
"Listen! Three things. Which one is a lie? 1, 2, or 3? Show with your fingers!"

**Why:** Low-pressure start, establishes English-only environment, introduces "I'm from..."

---

### **[8-13 MIN] VOCABULARY PRESENTATION**

**Write on board:**
```
GREETINGS
Hello!
Hi!
Good morning
Good afternoon
Good evening
Nice to meet you
Goodbye
See you
See you later
```

**Teaching Steps:**

1. **Point to each word** (don't say yet)
2. **Mime/gesture** the meaning
   - "Hello!" - wave hand
   - "Goodbye!" - wave hand going away
   - "Good morning" - point to sun/window
   - "Nice to meet you" - shake hands with self
3. **Say the word** (learners listen)
4. **Choral repetition:** All learners repeat 3x
   - Teacher: "Hello!" → Class: "Hello! Hello! Hello!"
5. **Individual repetition:** Point to random learners
   - "Ahmed, say 'Good morning'"
   - Praise: "Good! Excellent!"

**Key Points:**
- Do NOT translate to French yet
- Use gesture + mime
- Exaggerate lip movement
- Smile and be energetic

**Common Learner Difficulty:**
- "Good morning" vs "Good afternoon" (time confusion)
- Solution: Point to clock or window to show time

---

### **[13-18 MIN] ALPHABET DRILL**

**Present Alphabet in Sound Groups** (not A-B-C order)

**Write on board:**
```
Group 1: A-H-J-K ("ay" sound)
Group 2: B-C-D-E-G-P-T-V ("bee" sound)  
Group 3: F-L-M-N-S-X-Z ("letter" sound)
Group 4: I-Y ("aye" sound)
Group 5: O ("oh" sound)
Group 6: Q-U-W ("you" sound)
Group 7: R (special!)
```

**Teaching Steps:**

1. **Say Group 1** (A, H, J, K) - all have same "ay" sound
2. **Model:** Exaggerate mouth shape "AAAYYY"
3. **Choral repetition:** All say together 3x
4. **Individual:** Call on learners randomly
5. **Repeat for each group**
6. **Activity: Spell Names**
   - Say: "Ahmed! Spell your name!"
   - Ahmed: "A-H-M-E-D"
   - Class repeats: "A-H-M-E-D!"
   - Praise: "Perfect!"

**Do this for 3-4 learner names**

**Why:** French people struggle with English alphabet grouping. This helps!

---

### **[18-23 MIN] GRAMMAR: TO BE (I am/I'm)**

**Write on board:**
```
I am Maria.
I am from Spain.
I am a teacher.

= SHORTER WAY =
I'm Maria.
I'm from Spain.
I'm a teacher.
```

**Teaching Steps:**

1. **Model sentence 1:** "I am Maria. → I'm Maria."
   - Point to self: "I"
   - Gesture to mean "I am"
   - Say name: "Maria"
   - Cross out "am" → write "'m"

2. **Do 2-3 more examples on board**
   - "I am from Spain" → "I'm from Spain"
   - "I am a teacher" → "I'm a teacher"

3. **Choral repetition:** Whole class repeats 3x
   - Teacher: "I'm Maria!"
   - Class: "I'm Maria! I'm Maria! I'm Maria!"

4. **Individual practice:**
   - Point to learner: "Say: I'm [learner's name]"
   - Learner: "I'm Ahmed!"
   - Praise: "Excellent!"

5. **Build the full sentence:**
   - Teacher: "I'm Maria. I'm from Spain. I'm a teacher."
   - Class repeats (3x)

**Grammar Note:** 
- "am" = short form with "I"
- Only teach "I am/I'm" today (not he/she yet)
- Contraction ('m) is critical for French speakers

---

### **[23-33 MIN] PAIR WORK: INTRODUCTIONS**

**Activity: Information Gap — Complete ID Cards**

**Materials:**
- Blank ID cards (template on board or printed)

**ID Card Template:**
```
NAME: ________________
COUNTRY: ________________
JOB: ________________
```

**Setup:**
- Divide class into pairs
- Pair A: Gets card with NAME and COUNTRY filled in (JOB blank)
- Pair B: Gets card with NAME and JOB filled in (COUNTRY blank)

**Instructions to Learners:**
"You have a card. Talk with your partner. Ask questions. Fill the blanks!"

**Model the dialogue FIRST:**

**Teacher + Confident Learner:**
- You: "What's your name?"
- Learner: "My name is Ahmed!"
- You: "Where are you from?"
- Learner: "I'm from Egypt!"
- You: "What do you do?"
- Learner: "I'm an engineer!"

**Then say:**
"Now YOU do this with your partner. Go!"

**Monitor:**
- Circulate while pairs work
- Listen for correct use of "I'm"
- Note errors (don't correct, just listen)
- Encourage: "Good! Keep going!"

**After 5 minutes:** "Swap cards! Now fill the OTHER blanks!"

**Total time:** 10 minutes (5 min each direction)

---

### **[33-43 MIN] GROUP ACTIVITY: SPEED NETWORKING**

**Activity: Introductions Game**

**Setup:**
- Divide into groups of 6
- Stand in circle
- Each person has their completed card

**Instructions:**
"Each person introduces themselves. 30 seconds each. Say: My name is... I'm from... I'm a..."

**Model FIRST (with yourself):**
"Listen to me. My name is Teacher [Name]. I'm from [Country]. I'm a teacher. Now YOU go!"

**Process:**
1. **Person 1** introduces (30 sec)
2. **Person 2** introduces (30 sec)
3. Continue around circle (6 people = 3 minutes per round)
4. **Second round:** Different group asks: "What's your name?"
5. Person responds quickly

**Challenge (for fast learners):**
"Can you remember everyone's name?"

**After activity:**
"Did anyone remember all the names? Who can say them?"

**Why:** Repetition with meaning. Public speaking practice. Confidence building.

---

### **[43-50 MIN] LISTENING ACTIVITY**

**Audio: Introduction Dialogue (2 min, very slow pace)**

**Suggested Dialogue:**
```
A: Hello! What's your name?
B: Hi! My name is Maria. Where are you from?
A: I'm from Canada. I'm a teacher. What do you do?
B: I'm a nurse. I'm from Spain.
A: Nice to meet you, Maria!
B: Nice to meet you too!
```

**Play audio 1st time:** Learners just listen (no tasks)

**Comprehension Questions (ask in English, accept 1-word answers):**
1. "What's her name?" → Answer: "Maria"
2. "Where's Maria from?" → "Spain"
3. "What does she do?" → "Nurse"
4. "What's his job?" → "Teacher"

**Play audio 2nd time:** Learners listen again

**Repeat Questions:** Can they remember?

**Why:** Develops listening comprehension. Vocabulary reinforcement. Natural speech rhythm.

---

### **[50-55 MIN] WRITING & EXIT TICKET**

**Activity: Write Your Introduction**

**Instructions:**
"Write 3 sentences. About YOU."

**Model on board:**
```
My name is [Your Name].
I'm from [Your Country].
I'm a [Your Job].
```

**Learners write their own version** (5 min)

**Collect as "Ticket of Exit":**
- Learners must give you their paper before leaving
- You check what they wrote
- No judgment — just assess understanding

**What you're checking:**
✓ Can they write their name?
✓ Do they use "I'm from..."?
✓ Do they use "I'm a..."?

### **[55-60 MIN] HOMEWORK & GOODBYE**

**Explain Homework:**

**Homework (Due before Session 2):**

**VOCABULARY:**

**LISTENING:**

**SPEAKING:**

"Stand up. Everyone! Look at me."

**Say:** "See you next time! Say it!"

**Class:** "See you next time!"

**Repeat 2x**

**Wave goodbye:** "Goodbye! Goodbye!"

**Why:** Repetition of goodbye phrase. Positive ending. Sets expectation for Session 2.

## 🔍 COMMON PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS

| Problem | Why It Happens | Solution |
|---------|---|---|
| Learners don't speak | Shy, afraid of mistakes | Pair work, gesture, model first |
| Can't pronounce "th" in "the" | French doesn't have this sound | Use mirror exercise, show tongue |
| Confused about "am/is/are" | Haven't seen grammar yet | Say "I'm" only (not full paradigm today) |
| Too quiet to hear | Class noise | Use hand signal (raise hand) to get quiet |
| Finished early | Faster learners | Give them extra names to spell |

---

## 💡 TEACHER NOTES

✓ **Don't translate to French** — use gesture instead  
✓ **Praise effort, not perfection** — "Good try! Almost!"  
✓ **Go SLOW** — allow thinking time  
✓ **Repetition is good** — not boring for beginners  
✓ **Have fun** — your energy matters!  
✓ **Don't correct every error** — just model correct form
