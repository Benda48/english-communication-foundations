# Week 2: Personal Information

## Overview

**Theme:** Giving and requesting personal information (phone, email, address, age)  
**Grammar Focus:** Possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her); Have got; Articles a/an  
**Vocabulary:** Numbers 1-100, countries (revision), colors, ages, phone numbers  
**Pronunciation Focus:** /θ/ and /ð/ sounds (this, that, three); word stress in numbers  
**Hours:** 4 sessions × 1 hour = 4 hours  

---

## Can-Do Statements

By the end of this week, learners will be able to:

✓ Give phone number, email, address  
✓ Spell their name in English  
✓ Ask and answer questions about age  
✓ Understand possessive adjectives  
✓ Fill in a simple form  
✓ Use numbers correctly (stress and pronunciation)

## Grammar Reference — Week 2

### Possessive Adjectives

| Subject Pronoun | Possessive Adjective | Example |
|-----------------|----------------------|---------|
| I | My | My name is Maria |
| You | Your | Your phone number? |
| He | His | His job is teacher |
| She | Her | Her country is Spain |
| It | Its | (rarely used in A1) |
| We | Our | Our school is big |
| They | Their | Their ages? |

**Important:** Possessive adjectives do NOT change with singular/plural nouns.
- My name (singular)
- My books (plural)

### Have got (Present Simple)

**Affirmative:**
- I have got a phone / I've got a phone
- She has got an email / She's got an email
- They have got a car / They've got a car

**Negative:**
- I haven't got a phone
- He hasn't got a car

**Question:**
- Have you got a phone? → Yes, I have / No, I haven't
- Has she got an email? → Yes, she has / No, she hasn't

### Articles (a/an)

- **a** + consonant sound: a phone, a teacher, a university
- **an** + vowel sound: an email, an apple, an hour

## Session Breakdown — Week 2

### SESSION 5: Numbers & Phone

**Objectives:**
- Count 1-100
- Pronounce numbers correctly (stress)
- Give and ask for phone numbers

**Materials:**
- Number flashcards
- Telephone dialogue audio
- Form template

**Key Activity: Number Telephone Game**
Teacher calls out a phone number slowly.
Learners write it down.
Swap: Learners call out numbers, others write.

**Pronunciation Focus:**
- THIRteen vs THIRTY
- FIFteen vs FIFTY
- Isolation drill: Minimal pairs

---

### SESSION 6: Personal Details Form

**Objectives:**
- Complete a form with personal information
- Use possessive adjectives
- Spell names

**Materials:**
- Blank forms
- Alphabet reference cards

**Key Activity: Information Gap**
Pair A: Has form with some blanks
Pair B: Has different blanks
Must ask each other: "What's your name? How do you spell it? Your phone number?"

---

### SESSION 7: Age & Birthday

**Objectives:**
- Ask and say age
- Say birthday (month + date)
- Understand months

**Materials:**
- Calendar visual
- Age/birthday cards

**Key Activity: Age Interview**
Walk around asking: "How old are you? When's your birthday?"
Note responses, then report back: "Maria is 25. Her birthday is March 15."

---

### SESSION 8: Review & Forms

**Objectives:**
- Consolidate all Week 2
- Complete realistic form (job application, registration)

**Key Activity: Real Form Challenge**
Provide actual simple registration form (school, gym, online shop).
Learners complete it working in pairs.
