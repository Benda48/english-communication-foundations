# Week 4: Daily Routine

## Overview

**Theme:** Describing daily activities and routines  
**Grammar Focus:** Present simple (affirmative, negative, interrogative); Frequency adverbs  
**Vocabulary:** Routine verbs, times, days of week  
**Pronunciation Focus:** /h/ aspiration; -s/-es verb endings  
**Hours:** 4 sessions × 1 hour = 4 hours  

---

## Can-Do Statements

By the end of this week, learners will be able to:

✓ Describe their daily routine  
✓ Use present simple correctly  
✓ Ask about someone's routine  
✓ Use frequency adverbs (always, usually, often, sometimes, never)

## Grammar Reference — Week 4

### Present Simple

**Affirmative:**
- I/You/We/They wake up at 7am
- He/She/It wakes up at 7am (note: -s ending)

**Negative:**
- I don't wake up early
- She doesn't wake up early (note: does not = doesn't)

**Question:**
- Do you wake up early? → Yes, I do / No, I don't
- Does she wake up early? → Yes, she does / No, she doesn't

**Verb Ending Rules:**
- Regular: add -s (wake → wakes)
- After s/x/z/ch/sh: add -es (watch → watches, wash → washes)
- After consonant + y: change y to -ies (study → studies)
- After vowel + y: add -s (play → plays)

### Frequency Adverbs Placement

**Position:** Between subject and verb
- I always wake up at 7am
- She usually comes home at 5pm
- They often watch TV
- We sometimes go to the cinema
- He never eats breakfast

## Session Breakdown — Week 4

### SESSION 13: My Typical Day

**Objectives:**
- Describe routine using present simple
- Use time expressions
- Conjugate 3rd person singular

**Key Activity: Timeline**
Teacher draws timeline on board: 6am → 11pm
Places routine activities at times
Learners copy and fill in their own timeline
Describe: "I wake up at 6am. I have breakfast at 6:30."

---

### SESSION 14: Do/Does Questions

**Objectives:**
- Form questions with do/does
- Answer affirmatively/negatively
- Use frequency adverbs

**Key Activity: Survey**
"Do you wake up before 7am? Do you have breakfast?"
Each learner asks 5 classmates
Reports: "Maria usually wakes up at 8am. Kofi always has breakfast."

---

### SESSION 15: Frequency Adverbs

**Objectives:**
- Use frequency adverbs correctly
- Position in sentence

**Key Activity: Frequency Line**
Draw line: Never ←→ Always
Statement: "I eat breakfast"
Learners place themselves on line + explain position

---

### SESSION 16: REVIEW + MID-COURSE ASSESSMENT

**Objectives:**
- Consolidate Weeks 1-4
- Formal mid-course evaluation

**Assessment (30 min):**
- Listening: 2 conversations, 5 Q&A
