## Grammar Reference — Week 8

### Like/Love/Hate + V-ing

**Structure:** Like/Love/Hate + [verb + -ing]
- I like reading
- She loves playing tennis
- They hate studying
- Do you like swimming? → Yes, I do / No, I don't

**V-ing formation:**
- read → reading
- play → playing
- swim → swimming
- watch → watching
- study → studying (double final consonant after short vowel)

### Want to + Verb

**Near future intentions:**
- I want to learn English
- She wants to be a teacher
- Do you want to travel? → Yes, I do / No, I don't

**Negative:**
- I don't want to work tomorrow
- He doesn't want to study

### Going to + Verb (Introduction)

**Future plans (within 1 week):**
- I'm going to visit my family next weekend
- Are you going to watch the match? → Yes, I am / No, I'm not
- They're going to travel next month

## Session Breakdown — Week 8

### SESSION 29: Hobbies & Interests

**Objectives:**
- Learn hobbies vocabulary
- Use Like/Love/Hate + V-ing
- Ask about interests

**Key Activity: Hobby Interview**
Walk around asking: "What do you like doing? Do you love sports?"
Take notes
Report to class: "Maria likes reading. Kofi loves football."

---

### SESSION 30: Work & Career Dreams

**Objectives:**
- Describe current job
- Talk about dream job
- Use Want to

**Key Activity: Career Board**
Write on board: "What do you do now? What do you want to do?"
Learners share: "I'm a student now. I want to be an engineer."

---

### SESSION 31: Plans & Aspirations

**Objectives:**
- Use going to + V
- Talk about short/long-term plans
- Express ambitions

**Key Activity: My Week Ahead**
Learners write 3 things they're going to do this week
Share: "I'm going to visit my family. I'm going to study."

---

### SESSION 32: FINAL CELEBRATION & ASSESSMENT

**Objectives:**
- Celebrate completion of program
- Formal final assessment (45 min)
- Reflection and feedback

**Final Assessment Components:**

| Component | Duration | Assessment Type |
|-----------|----------|------------------|
| **Listening** | 8 min | 3 recordings, 5-8 Q&A |
| **Speaking** | 3 min | Personal presentation (topic of choice) |
| **Interaction** | 5 min | Role play (full scenario: restaurant/shopping/directions) |
| **Reading** | 7 min | 80-word text, 5 comprehension Q |
| **Writing** | 10 min | Email/Message 60-80 words to pen pal |

**Post-Assessment:**
- Celebrate achievements
- Share best moments
- Photo/video celebrations
- Certificates of completion
- Reflection discussion: "How has your English improved?"
