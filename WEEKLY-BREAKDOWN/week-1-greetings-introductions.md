# Week 1: Greetings & Introductions

## Overview

**Theme:** Meeting people, introducing yourself, saying hello and goodbye  
**Grammar Focus:** To be (am/is/are) — affirmative, negative, interrogative; contractions  
**Vocabulary:** Greetings, names, professions, countries, nationalities  
**Pronunciation Focus:** Alphabet, vowel sounds /æ/ /ɪ/ /ʌ/  
**Hours:** 4 sessions × 1 hour = 4 hours  

---

## Can-Do Statements (CECRL A1)

By the end of this week, learners will be able to:

✓ Greet people and say goodbye in English  
✓ Introduce themselves (name, country, job)  
✓ Understand simple introduction questions  
✓ Ask someone their name and where they are from  
✓ Use "to be" in simple sentences

## Grammar Reference — Week 1

### To Be (Present Simple)

#### Affirmative Form

| Person | Full Form | Contraction |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| I | I am | I'm |
| You | You are | You're |
| He | He is | He's |
| She | She is | She's |
| It | It is | It's |
| We | We are | We're |
| They | They are | They're |

**Examples:**
- I am a teacher. (I'm a teacher.)
- She is from France. (She's from France.)
- They are students. (They're students.)

#### Negative Form

| Person | Full Form | Contraction |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| I | I am not | I'm not |
| You | You are not | You're not / You ain't |
| He/She | He is not | He's not / He isn't |
| We/They | We are not | We're not / We aren't |

**Examples:**
- I'm not from Spain. I'm from France.
- He isn't a doctor. He's an engineer.

#### Interrogative Form

**Yes/No Questions:**
- Are you a student? → Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
- Is she from Italy? → Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
- Are they doctors? → Yes, they are. / No, they're not.

**Wh-Questions (Introduction):**
- What's your name? (What is = What's)
- Where are you from?
- What do you do? (job)

## Session Breakdown

### SESSION 1: Hello! My name is...

**Objectives:**
- Greet and introduce yourself
- Use "I am" correctly
- Pronounce the alphabet

**Materials:**
- Blank name cards
- Audio recording: Introduction dialogue
- Alphabet poster

**Timing:**

| Time | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| 0-3 min | **Greeting at door** — Teacher greets each learner individually | 3 min |
| 3-8 min | **Icebreaker** — Board: "Hello / Hi / Good morning" | 5 min |
| 8-13 min | **Vocabulary presentation** — Greetings + repetition | 5 min |
| 13-18 min | **Alphabet drill** — Grouped by sounds | 5 min |
| 18-23 min | **Grammar** — "I am / I'm" model & practice | 5 min |
| 23-33 min | **Pair work** — Speed introductions with name cards | 10 min |
| 33-43 min | **Group activity** — Speed networking (6-person groups) | 10 min |
| 43-50 min | **Listening** — Audio dialogue + comprehension Q | 7 min |
| 50-55 min | **Writing** — "Ticket of exit" (3 sentences self-intro) | 5 min |
| 55-60 min | **Homework & goodbye** — Explain assignment, say goodbye in English | 5 min |

**Detailed Instructions:**

**[0-3] Greeting at door:**
"Hello! I'm [name]. What's your name?"
Note reactions, help learners respond.

**[3-8] Icebreaker:**
Write on board: Hello / Hi / Good morning / Good afternoon / Good evening
Ask: "What do these mean?" Gesture and mime.

**[8-13] Vocabulary:**
Add to board: "Nice to meet you," "Goodbye," "See you"
Choral repetition 3× each word
Individual repetition (random learner selection)

**[13-18] Alphabet:**
Present in groups of similar sounds:
- A-H-J-K ("ay")
- B-C-D-E-G-P-T-V ("bee")
- Etc.
Echo drill. Spell names of learners.

**[18-23] Grammar:**
Board: "I am Amara. I am from Côte d'Ivoire. I am a student."
Replace info. Show contraction: "I am → I'm"
Mini-drill: Learners repeat after you.

**[23-33] Pair work:**
Each learner receives blank name card.
Write: first name, country, job
Rotate every 2 minutes. Model first: "Hi! My name is [name]. I'm from [country]. I'm a [job]. What's your name?"

**[33-43] Speed networking:**
Groups of 6 standing. Each introduces to all members (30 sec each).
Group chooses best introduction, presents to class.

**[43-50] Listening:**
Play audio: Two people introducing themselves (2 min, slow pace)
Questions: Name? Country? Job?
Re-play if needed.

**[50-55] Writing:**
Each learner writes 3 sentences: "My name is... I'm from... I'm a..."
Submit on exit.

**[55-60] Homework:**
Explain Week 1 Homework
Word of the day: "INTRODUCE"
Final goodbye: "See you next time!"

**Teacher Notes:**
- First session is crucial. Maintain high energy, positive climate.
- Value ALL attempts, even imperfect ones.
- Do NOT correct minor grammar errors today. Focus on communication.
- Build confidence — learners will be anxious.

## Differentiation Notes

**For Struggling Learners:**
- Pair with strong learner
- Provide sentence starters
- Reduce complexity: Only name + country, not job yet
- Extra repetition and modeling

**For Advanced Learners:**
- Add more professions
- Introduce questions: "How are you?"
- Ask follow-up questions
- Use more complex sentences
