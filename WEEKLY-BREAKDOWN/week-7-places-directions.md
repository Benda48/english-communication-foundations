# Week 7: Places & Directions

## Overview

**Theme:** Navigating cities, asking for and giving directions  
**Grammar Focus:** Imperatives; Prepositions of place; There is/are (revision)  
**Vocabulary:** Public places, movement verbs, street/location terms  
**Pronunciation Focus:** Rhythm groups; Question intonation for directions  
**Hours:** 4 sessions × 1 hour = 4 hours  

---

## Can-Do Statements

By the end of this week, learners will be able to:

✓ Ask for directions  
✓ Give simple directions  
✓ Describe locations (next to, opposite, between)  
✓ Use imperatives (Turn, Go, Take)  
✓ Follow GPS-style instructions  
✓ Describe a route from A to B

## Grammar Reference — Week 7

### Imperatives

**Formation:** Verb (base form) without subject
- Go straight
- Turn left
- Take the first street
- Cross the bridge
- Continue for 200 meters

**Negative:**
- Don't turn right
- Don't go straight

### Prepositions + Locations

- The bank is **next to** the supermarket
- The school is **opposite** the hospital
- The cinema is **between** the post office and the café
- The park is **on** Main Street
- The hotel is **near** the station

## Session Breakdown — Week 7

### SESSION 25: Places in Town

**Objectives:**
- Learn public places
- Understand locations
- Use prepositions

**Key Activity: Town Map**
Teacher shows/draws map with labeled places
Learners ask: "Where's the bank?"
Teacher responds: "Next to the supermarket, opposite the hospital"

---

### SESSION 26: Imperatives & Directions

**Objectives:**
- Give simple direction commands
- Use imperatives correctly
- Understand GPS language

**Key Activity: Physical Directions**
Teacher gives imperatives; learners move
"Go forward. Turn left. Turn right. Stop!"
Then learners give imperatives to teacher

---

### SESSION 27: Asking & Giving Directions

**Objectives:**
- Full direction exchange
- Ask politely
- Describe routes

**Key Activity: Direction Pairs**
Pair A: Lost tourist (has destination)
Pair B: Local (has map)
Tourist: "Excuse me, where's the bank?"
Local: "Go straight, turn left, it's on your right"
Rotate

---

### SESSION 28: Navigation Challenge

**Objectives:**
- Review and apply
- Speed directional activity

**Key Activity: "Find It" Game**
Learners receive simple map + written destination
Must ask classmates for directions
"Excuse me, where is...?"
Write down directions
Then explain back to teacher
