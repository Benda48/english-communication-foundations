# Week 6: Shopping

## Overview

**Theme:** Buying clothes and negotiating prices  
**Grammar Focus:** How much/How many; Can (ability/permission); Descriptive adjectives  
**Vocabulary:** Clothing, colors, sizes, prices, payment terms  
**Pronunciation Focus:** Diphthongs /eɪ/ and /oʊ/; Price pronunciation  
**Hours:** 4 sessions × 1 hour = 4 hours  

---

## Can-Do Statements

By the end of this week, learners will be able to:

✓ Ask for prices (How much is it?)  
✓ Describe clothing (color, size, style)  
✓ Make simple purchases  
✓ Ask permission/ability with "Can"  
✓ Negotiate simple prices  
✓ Understand payment methods

## Grammar Reference — Week 6

### How much / How many

**How much** (uncountable):
- How much does it cost?
- How much is the shirt?
- How much is the water?

**How many** (countable plural):
- How many shirts?
- How many people?
- How many students?

### Can (Ability/Permission)

**Ability:**
- I can swim
- Can you speak English?

**Permission:**
- Can I try it on?
- Can I pay by card?
- Can we look around?

**Negative:**
- I can't afford it
- We can't wear that here

## Session Breakdown — Week 6

### SESSION 21: Clothes & Descriptions

**Objectives:**
- Learn clothing vocabulary
- Describe with color + size
- Ask "Can I...?"

**Key Activity: Clothing Bingo**
Call out: "Blue shirt, size large"
Learners mark on card if they're wearing similar
First with line wins

---

### SESSION 22: Prices & How much?

**Objectives:**
- Ask about prices
- Use correct diphthong pronunciation
- Understand payment methods

**Key Activity: Price Haggle**
Teacher shows item with high price
Learners ask: "How much? Can you reduce it?"
Teacher negotiates playfully

---

### SESSION 23: Shopping Role Play

**Objectives:**
- Full shopping transaction
- Multiple items
- Request try-on
- Pay and receive change

**Key Activity: Clothing Shop**
A: Customer (has list of 5 items to buy)
B: Vendor
Customer describes what they want
Vendor shows items
Negotiate price
Pay
Rotate

---

### SESSION 24: Market Madness

**Objectives:**
- Review all Week 6
- Speed transactions
- Fun consolidation

**Key Activity: "English Market Day"**
5-7 vendor stations (different product types)
Learners rotate with money budget
Must buy 3-4 items before "market closes"
All in English
