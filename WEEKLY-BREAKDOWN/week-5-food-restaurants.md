## Grammar Reference — Week 5

### Would like (I'd like)

**Polite requests:**
- I'd like a coffee (I would like)
- Would you like tea? (formal polite question)
- I'd like some water, please

**Contraction:** would → 'd
- I'd = I would
- You'd = You would
- She'd = She would

**Usage:**
- I'd like [singular countable] — I'd like a coffee
- I'd like [uncountable] — I'd like water
- I'd like [plural] — I'd like some cookies

### Some vs Any

**Some** (affirmative sentences):
- I'd like some water
- There are some apples
- I have some money

**Any** (negative & questions):
- Do you have any tea?
- I don't have any sugar
- Is there any bread?

## Session Breakdown — Week 5

### SESSION 17: Food Vocabulary & Preferences

**Objectives:**
- Learn food words
- Express preferences
- Use "like" and "don't like"

**Key Activity: Food Picture Game**
Show images of foods
Learners say: "I like chicken / I don't like fish"
Class asks: "Do you like...?"

---

### SESSION 18: Restaurant Dialogue

**Objectives:**
- Learn restaurant phrases
- Understand "Would like"
- Use "Please" and "Thank you"

**Key Activity: Listen & Repeat**
Audio of waiter + customer
Key phrases:
- "Welcome to..." / "Table for two?"
- "I'd like..."
- "Anything to drink?"
- "How was your meal?"
- "The bill, please"

---

### SESSION 19: Role Play Restaurant

**Objectives:**
- Conduct full restaurant transaction
- Order for multiple people
- Ask about menu items

**Key Activity: Restaurant Pairs**
Role A: Server (has menu, writes order, brings bill)
Role B: Customer (orders, asks questions)
Items have prices
Server calculates total
Rotate after 10 min

---

### SESSION 20: Market Simulation

**Objectives:**
- Review and consolidate
- Handle money
- Negotiation basics

**Key Activity: "English Food Market"**
Multiple vendor stations (pizza stand, sandwich bar, juice bar, dessert stall)
Each learner is vendor + customer
Rotate stations
All interactions in English
