# Week 3: Family & Friends

## Overview

**Theme:** Describing family members and relationships  
**Grammar Focus:** There is/There are; Adjectives for description; Plural nouns  
**Vocabulary:** Family members, physical descriptions, personality traits  
**Pronunciation Focus:** Liaisons (He's, She's, They're); Question intonation  
**Hours:** 4 sessions × 1 hour = 4 hours  

---

## Can-Do Statements

By the end of this week, learners will be able to:

✓ Describe their family  
✓ Use "There is/are" to describe groups  
✓ Describe someone's appearance (hair, eyes, size)  
✓ Talk about family relationships  
✓ Ask about someone's family  
✓ Use simple adjectives

## Grammar Reference — Week 3

### There is / There are

**Affirmative:**
- There is a mother → There's a mother
- There are two brothers → There're two brothers

**Negative:**
- There is not a sister → There isn't a sister
- There are not children → There aren't children

**Question:**
- Is there a father? → Yes, there is / No, there isn't
- Are there sisters? → Yes, there are / No, there aren't

**Usage:** Describing what exists in a group/place
- There is a dog in my house
- There are five people in my family
- There aren't any siblings

### Adjectives in English

**Order:** Article + Adjective + Noun
- A tall man
- The small dog
- Two friendly sisters

**Multiple adjectives:** Size + Color + Noun
- A big brown dog
- Three small black cats

## Session Breakdown — Week 3

### SESSION 9: My Family

**Objectives:**
- Name family members
- Describe basic family
- Use "There is/are"

**Key Activity: Family Tree Drawing**
Teacher describes own family using visual.
Learners draw their own family tree.
Then describe to partner: "I have a mother, father, two sisters..."

---

### SESSION 10: Descriptions

**Objectives:**
- Describe appearance
- Use adjectives
- Understand descriptions

**Key Activity: Picture Difference Game**
Pair A: Image of family with blonde father, tall mother, short children
Pair B: Image with brown-haired father, short mother, tall children
Describe: "In my picture, the father has... The mother is..."
Find differences

---

### SESSION 11: There is/There are

**Objectives:**
- Use existence structure
- Ask about family composition

**Key Activity: Family Questionnaire**
"In your family, is there a dog? Are there children? Are there grandparents?"
Answers: "Yes, there is..." / "No, there isn't..."

---

### SESSION 12: Review & Presentations

**Objectives:**
- Consolidate Week 3
- Present family to class

**Key Activity: Family Poster**
Learners bring photo or draw family.
Present: "In my family, there are five people. My mother is..."
Class asks questions: "Is there a sister?"
