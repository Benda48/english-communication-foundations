# Complete Curriculum Document

## SECTION 1: OVERVIEW & ALIGNMENT

### Program Description

English Communication Foundations is an intensive 8-week English oral communication program designed for francophone adult beginners (absolute beginners to false beginners) seeking to acquire functional communication capacity in English rapidly.

The program is based on principles of Communicative Language Teaching (CLT), Task-Based Learning (TBL), and andragogy (adult learning theory) to provide a dynamic, interactive, and immediately applicable learning experience.

**Target Level:** A1 Functional (CECRL)
**Duration:** 8 weeks | 32 sessions | 32 hours
**Class Size:** 30 learners
**Format:** 4 hours per week (4 x 1-hour sessions) + homework via WhatsApp

### Learning Objectives Summary

At the end of the program, learners will be able to:

1. **Introduce themselves** with name, nationality, profession, age
2. **Give and request personal information** (phone, email, address, etc.)
3. **Describe family and relationships** using simple descriptions
4. **Narrate daily routine** using present simple
5. **Interact in restaurants** (order, ask for bills)
6. **Shop** (ask prices, request sizes, negotiate simply)
7. **Navigate urban spaces** (ask for and give directions)
8. **Express hobbies and future plans** using basic formulas

### CECRL Alignment Matrix

| CECRL Competency | A1 Can-Do Statements | Sessions | Assessment |
|------------------|----------------------|----------|------------|
| **Listening** | Understand short, slow sentences on familiar topics | All | Quiz + practice |
| **Reading** | Understand names, words, very simple phrases | S3-S8 | Reading exercises |
| **Speaking** | Introduce self, ask simple personal questions | S1-S8 | Speaking rubric |
| **Interaction** | Simple interaction if speaker is slow and clear | S2-S8 | Role plays |
| **Writing** | Write postcards, fill simple forms | S2-S8 | Writing tasks |

## SECTION 2: 8-WEEK PROGRESSION

### Week 1: Greetings & Introductions

**Grammar:** To be (am/is/are) — affirmative, negative, interrogative; contractions
**Vocabulary:** Greetings, names, professions, countries, nationalities
**Pronunciation Focus:** Alphabet, vowel sounds /æ/ /ɪ/ /ʌ/
**Can-Do Statements:**
- I can greet people appropriately
- I can introduce myself (name, country, job)
- I can understand simple introduction questions
- I can ask someone their name

**Key Activities:**
- Ice-breaker: Speed introductions
- Pair work: "Who are you?" information gap
- Whole class: Speed networking
- Listening: Dialogue between strangers
- Writing: Fill ID card with personal info

**Materials Needed:**
- Blank ID cards
- Audio recording: Introduction dialogue
- Name badges
- Vocabulary poster

---

### Week 2: Personal Information

**Grammar:** Possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her); Have got; Articles a/an
**Vocabulary:** Numbers 1-100, countries, ages, phone numbers, email, addresses
**Pronunciation Focus:** /θ/ and /ð/ sounds (this, that, three), word stress
**Can-Do Statements:**
- I can give my phone number
- I can spell my name in English
- I can state my age and nationality
- I can fill in a form with personal details

**Key Activities:**
- Number games and telephone dialogues
- Spelling competition
- Form-filling practice
- Listening: Telephonic conversation
- Writing: Personal profile (5 sentences)

---

### Week 3: Family & Friends

**Grammar:** There is/There are; Adjectives (big, small, young, old); Plural nouns
**Vocabulary:** Family members, descriptions (hair, eyes), personality traits
**Pronunciation Focus:** Liaisons (He's, She's, They're); Question intonation
**Can-Do Statements:**
- I can describe my family
- I can describe a person's appearance
- I can use "There is/are" correctly
- I can talk about family relationships

**Key Activities:**
- Family tree drawing
- Photo descriptions
- Information gap: "Find differences"
- Listening: Audio description of family
- Writing: Paragraph about own family

---

### Week 4: Daily Routine

**Grammar:** Present simple (affirmative, negative, interrogative); Frequency adverbs
**Vocabulary:** Routine verbs (wake up, get up, go to bed, work, eat); Times; Days
**Pronunciation Focus:** /h/ aspiration; -s/-es verb endings
**Can-Do Statements:**
- I can describe my daily routine
- I can use frequency adverbs correctly
- I can ask about someone's routine
- I can tell time

**Key Activities:**
- Timeline activity: Order actions
- Routine interview in pairs
- Class survey: Common routines
- Listening: Person describing their day
- Writing: Paragraph on personal routine

**Mid-Course Assessment (End of Week 4)**
- Listening comprehension: 2 conversations, 5 Q&A
- Speaking monologue: Describe routine (3 min)
- Interaction: Role play with teacher
- Written vocabulary matching
- Short written production: 5 sentences

---

### Week 5: Food & Restaurants

**Grammar:** Would like; Some/Any; Countable/Uncountable nouns (introduction)
**Vocabulary:** Foods, drinks, tastes (delicious, spicy), menu terms
**Pronunciation Focus:** /w/ and /v/ sounds; Phrase stress
**Can-Do Statements:**
- I can order at a restaurant politely
- I can express food preferences
- I can understand a menu
- I can ask for a bill

**Key Activities:**
- Restaurant role play (client & waiter)
- Menu reading activity
- Preference interview in pairs
- Listening: Restaurant dialogue
- Writing: Complete order for a group

---

### Week 6: Shopping

**Grammar:** How much/How many; Can (ability/permission); Adjectives (colors, sizes)
**Vocabulary:** Clothes, accessories, colors, sizes, prices, payment terms
**Pronunciation Focus:** Diphthongs /eɪ/ and /oʊ/; Price pronunciation
**Can-Do Statements:**
- I can ask for prices
- I can describe clothing
- I can negotiate (basic)
- I can make a simple purchase

**Key Activities:**
- Shop role play (customer & vendor)
- Clothing description game
- Market simulation (multiple stands)
- Listening: Shopping conversation
- Writing: Shopping list with details

---

### Week 7: Places & Directions

**Grammar:** Imperatives; Prepositions of place; There is/are (revision)
**Vocabulary:** Public places (bank, hospital, school); Movement verbs; Street terms
**Pronunciation Focus:** Rhythm groups; Question intonation for directions
**Can-Do Statements:**
- I can ask for directions
- I can give simple directions
- I can describe locations
- I can follow instructions

**Key Activities:**
- Direction-giving with maps
- GPS-like activity
- City exploration simulation
- Listening: GPS instruction
- Writing: Written directions

---

### Week 8: Hobbies, Work & Future Plans

**Grammar:** Like/Love/Hate + V-ing; Want to + V; Going to + V (introduction)
**Vocabulary:** Hobbies, sports, professions, responsibilities, future expressions
**Pronunciation Focus:** Full review; Natural speech rhythm; Contractions
**Can-Do Statements:**
- I can talk about my hobbies
- I can describe my work
- I can express future plans simply
- I can talk about preferences

**Key Activities:**
- Interview: Hobbies and interests
- Project presentation
- Career discussion
- Listening: Interview about hobbies & plans
- Writing: Letter to pen pal

**Final Assessment (End of Week 8)**
- Listening: 3 recordings, mixed QA formats
- Speaking presentation: Personal choice of topic (2-3 min)
- Interaction: Full role play scenario (restaurant/shopping/directions)
- Reading: 80-word text with 5 questions
- Writing: Email/WhatsApp message (60-80 words)

## SECTION 3: ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Diagnostic Assessment (Session 1)

**Purpose:** Identify actual level and adapt teaching

**Components:**
1. Conversation (2 min): Spontaneous oral level
2. Listening (3 min): 3 sentences → choose correct image
3. Writing (2 min): Write name, country, job
4. Self-assessment: Checklist "What can I do in English?"

**Outcome:** Teacher notes on individual cards for differentiation

### Mid-Course Assessment (Session 16, End Week 4)

**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Format:** Mixed skills

| Component | Duration | Weight | Format |
|-----------|----------|--------|--------|
| Listening | 5 min | 20% | 2 conversations, 5 true/false |
| Speaking | 3 min | 35% | Monologue: My routine |
| Interaction | 3 min | 25% | Role play: Simple meeting |
| Vocabulary | 5 min | 10% | Match 10 words to images |
| Writing | 7 min | 10% | 5 sentences about routine |

### Final Assessment (Session 32, End Week 8)

**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Format:** Comprehensive, all 4 skills + interaction

| Component | Duration | Weight | Format |
|-----------|----------|--------|--------|
| Listening | 8 min | 20% | 3 recordings, QA |
| Speaking | 3 min | 30% | Presentation (topic choice) |
| Interaction | 5 min | 25% | Full role play scenario |
| Reading | 7 min | 10% | 80-word text, 5 questions |
| Writing | 10 min | 15% | Email/Message 60-80 words |

### Speaking Rubric (6 Criteria × 5-Point Scale)

| Criterion | 5 - Excellent | 4 - Good | 3 - Satisfactory | 2 - Developing | 1 - Insufficient |
|-----------|---------------|---------|------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| **Fluency** | Natural, few pauses | Few pauses, fluent | Regular pauses | Very long pauses | Isolated words only |
| **Pronunciation** | Clear, understandable | Few errors | Some errors | Many errors | Incomprehensible |
| **Vocabulary** | Course vocab + extras | Appropriate | Basic, adequate | Limited | Insufficient |
| **Grammar** | Few errors, clear | Some errors | Frequent errors | Many errors | Systematic errors |
| **Comprehension** | Understands all | 1-2 repetitions | Slow/clear speech | Frequent reps | No comprehension |
| **Interaction** | Natural exchange | Responds well | Minimal replies | Very brief | No interaction |

**Total Score:** 30 points
- A1 Validated: 15+
- A1 with Merit: 21+
- A1 Exceptional: 27+
