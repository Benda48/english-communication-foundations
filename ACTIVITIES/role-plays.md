## ROLE PLAY 1: Meeting at a Conference

**Level:** Week 1-2  
**Duration:** 10-12 minutes  
**Objective:** Natural introduction + personal details exchange  

**Scenario:** You're both attending a professional conference. You don't know each other.

**Roles:**
- Person A: Wearing a name badge
- Person B: Approaching A to network

**Key Dialogue:**

B: "Hi! I'm Kofi. What's your name?"
A: "Hello, I'm Maria. Nice to meet you."
B: "Where are you from?"
A: "I'm from Spain. And you?"
B: "I'm from Ghana. What do you do?"
A: "I'm a teacher. And you?"
B: "I'm an engineer. Do you like your job?"
A: "Yes, very much. I love working with students."

**Follow-up:** Exchange contact information

## ROLE PLAY 2: Enrollment at Language School

**Level:** Week 2  
**Duration:** 10 minutes  
**Objective:** Fill in personal information accurately  

**Scenario:** Learner is enrolling in an English course.

**Roles:**
- Person A: Receptionist (has form)
- Person B: New student (wants to register)

**Key Dialogue:**

A: "Welcome to English Academy! Can I help you?"
B: "Yes, I'd like to register for an English course."
A: "Great! What's your full name?"
B: "My name is Amara Asante."
A: "How do you spell your first name?"
B: "A-M-A-R-A."
A: "Your phone number?"
B: "555-0123."
A: "And your email?"
B: "amara.asante at gmail.com."
A: "Perfect! Classes start Monday."

## Role Play Tips for Teachers

✓ **Model first:** Do role play with confident learner before pairs start  
✓ **Provide vocabulary:** Write key words on board  
✓ **Encourage spontaneity:** Allow improvisation beyond script  
✓ **Vary roles:** Let learners play both parts  
✓ **No perfection:** Accept hesitations, errors, repairs  
✓ **Extend complexity:** Add complications  
✓ **Celebrate:** Applaud after each role play
