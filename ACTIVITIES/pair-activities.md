# Pair Activities Bank\n## 15 Information-Gap and Communicative Pair Activities\n\n---\n\n## PAIR ACTIVITY 1: Who Are You?\n\n**Level:** Week 1-2 (Introductions & Personal Info)  \n**Duration:** 10-12 minutes  \n**Objective:** Practice introductions and personal information gathering  \n\n**Setup:**\n- Pair A: Has ID card template INCOMPLETE (missing: nationality, job, age)\n- Pair B: Has ID card template INCOMPLETE (different fields missing)\n\n**Instructions:**\nPartner A: \"What's your name?\"\nPartner B: \"My name is Maria. What's your name?\"\nPartner A: \"I'm Kofi. Where are you from?\"\nPartner B: \"I'm from Spain. Where are you from?\"\n\nContinue until both have completed cards.\n\n**Variation:** Use photos instead of blanks.\n\n---\n\n## PAIR ACTIVITY 2: Number Ping-Pong\n\n**Level:** Week 2 (Numbers)  \n**Duration:** 8-10 minutes  \n**Objective:** Practice number pronunciation (focus on 13 vs 30, 15 vs 50)  \n\n**Setup:**\nPartners sit facing each other\n\n**Instructions:**\nA says a number 1-100 (e.g., \"thirty-five\")\nB repeats it back\nB then says a new number\nA repeats it back\nContinue for 5 rounds each direction\n\n**Twist:** Learners take turns trying to confuse with similar numbers (13 vs 30, etc.)\n\n---\n\n## PAIR ACTIVITY 3: My Family Tree\n\n**Level:** Week 3 (Family)  \n**Duration:** 10-12 minutes  \n**Objective:** Describe family using There is/are and adjectives  \n\n**Setup:**\n- A has family tree with some names filled in\n- B has same tree but different names filled\n\n**Instructions:**\n\"In my family, there are five people. My mother is Anna. She's tall and has black hair. My father is...\"\n\nPartner B draws as they listen, then checks if drawing matches description.\n\n**Follow-up:** Swap roles. Then partner B describes their real family.\n\n---\n\n## PAIR ACTIVITY 4: What Time Do You...?\n\n**Level:** Week 4 (Daily Routine)  \n**Duration:** 10-12 minutes  \n**Objective:** Use present simple and time expressions  \n\n**Setup:**\n- A has schedule with times but activity names blank\n- B has activity names but times blank\n\n**Example:**\nA: \"What time does Sam wake up?\"\nB: \"Sam wakes up at 6:30am. What time does she have breakfast?\"\nA: \"She has breakfast at 7:15am. What activity happens at 12:00?\"\nB: \"She has lunch.\"\n\nContinue until both schedules are complete.\n\n---\n\n## PAIR ACTIVITY 5: Menu Ordering\n\n**Level:** Week 5 (Restaurants)  \n**Duration:** 12-15 minutes  \n**Objective:** Use polite requests (Would like) and restaurant dialogue  \n\n**Setup:**\n- A is the server (has menu with prices)\n- B is the customer (has no menu, budget limit)\n\n**Instructions:**\nB: \"Hello, I'd like a coffee and a sandwich, please.\"\nA: \"Of course! What sandwich? We have chicken, beef, or vegetarian.\"\nB: \"The chicken sandwich, please. How much is it?\"\nA: \"That's €8.50 for the sandwich and €2.50 for the coffee. Total €11.\"\nB: \"Can I pay by card?\"\nA: \"Of course!\"\n\nAfter 7 minutes, **swap roles**.\n\n---\n\n## PAIR ACTIVITY 6: How Much Is It?\n\n**Level:** Week 6 (Shopping)  \n**Duration:** 10-12 minutes  \n**Objective:** Ask for prices, negotiate, use Can I...?  \n\n**Setup:**\n- A: Vendor at a stall (has list of items with prices)\n- B: Customer (has budget, wants to buy 5 items)\n\n**Instructions:**\nB: \"How much is this shirt?\"\nA: \"It's €25.\"\nB: \"Can you reduce it?\"\nA: \"€22?\"\nB: \"Can I try it on?\"\nA: \"Yes, the fitting room is over there.\"\n\nB must buy 5 items within budget. Swap roles after 7 min.\n\n---\n\n## PAIR ACTIVITY 7: Where Is The Bank?\n\n**Level:** Week 7 (Directions)  \n**Duration:** 10-12 minutes  \n**Objective:** Ask for and give directions; use prepositions  \n\n**Setup:**\n- A: Has MAP 1 with some locations marked\n- B: Has MAP 2 with DIFFERENT locations marked (but same city layout)\n\n**Instructions:**\nA: \"Excuse me, where's the bank?\"\nB: \"The bank? Go straight. Turn left at the supermarket. It's opposite the hospital.\"\nA marks location on map.\n\nThen: \"Where's the cinema?\"\nB: \"The cinema is next to the hotel. Turn right at the corner.\"\n\nContinue until both maps are complete and match.\n\n---\n\n## PAIR ACTIVITY 8: Free Time Survey\n\n**Level:** Week 8 (Hobbies/Interests)  \n**Duration:** 10 minutes  \n**Objective:** Talk about hobbies using Like + V-ing and Want to  \n\n**Setup:**\nNo materials needed (or provide question cards)\n\n**Instructions:**\nPair A asks 6 questions:\n1. \"What do you like doing in your free time?\"\n2. \"Do you play sports?\"\n3. \"Do you like watching films? What genre?\"\n4. \"What do you want to learn?\"\n5. \"Do you have any dreams?\"\n6. \"What will you do this weekend?\"\n\nB answers. Then **swap roles**.\n\n**Follow-up:** Report to class: \"Maria likes reading. She wants to travel to Japan.\"\n\n---\n\n## Additional Pair Activities (9-15)\n\nSee the full curriculum document for Activities 9-15:\n- Activity 9: Describe The Person\n- Activity 10: Alphabet Race\n- Activity 11: Daily Schedule Bingo\n- Activity 12: Role Play Airport\n- Activity 13: Colour & Clothes\n- Activity 14: Favourite Food\n- Activity 15: Phone Message\n\n---\n\nEnd of Pair Activities"
