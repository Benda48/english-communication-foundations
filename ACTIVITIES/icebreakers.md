# Ice Breakers & Warm-Up Activities
## 10 Energizing Starter Activities for Class Opening

---

## ICE BREAKER 1: Two Truths, One Lie

**Level:** All weeks  
**Duration:** 5-7 minutes  
**Objective:** Energizer; practice speaking; learn about classmates  
**Materials:** None  

**Instructions:**
1. Each learner thinks of 3 statements about themselves (2 true, 1 false)
2. Example: "I have two sisters. I can swim. I love cooking."
3. Learner says all 3 statements
4. Class votes: Which one is a lie?
5. Reveal the truth

**Variation:** Teacher goes first to model, then each learner takes turns.

**Classroom Note:** Builds confidence and community.

---

## ICE BREAKER 2: Line Up!

**Level:** Week 2 (Numbers/Alphabet)  
**Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Objective:** Alphabet/number practice; physical activity  
**Materials:** None  

**Instructions:**
1. "Stand up! We're going to make a line."
2. "Line up in alphabetical order by first name. No talking!"
3. Once in line, go around and confirm order
4. "Now line up by age, youngest to oldest!"
5. "Now by birthday month!"

**Repeat 2-3 times with different criteria.**

**Variation:** Line up by country (A to Z), by job (A to Z).

---

## ICE BREAKER 3: Word of the Day Guess

**Level:** All weeks  
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Objective:** Vocabulary; mental activation  
**Materials:** None (or word written on paper/hidden)  

**Instructions:**
1. Teacher mimes or draws a word (no speaking)
2. Learners guess in English: "Is it...?"
3. Teacher confirms/denies
4. "Yes! It's [word]!"
5. Everyone repeats the word 3x

**Words:** Use vocabulary from current week.

---

## ICE BREAKER 4: Find Someone Who...

**Level:** All weeks  
**Duration:** 8-10 minutes  
**Objective:** Circulation; question practice; class bonding  
**Materials:** Questionnaire (printed or verbal)  

**Instructions:**
1. Each learner gets a card with items:
   - Find someone who has a brother
   - Find someone who likes pizza
   - Find someone who speaks 3 languages
   - Find someone who can swim
2. Learners walk around asking: "Do you have a brother? Do you like pizza?"
3. If yes, write that person's name
4. First to fill 5-8 squares wins

**Variation:** Make it competitive (team challenge).

---

## ICE BREAKER 5: Ball Toss Presentation

**Level:** Week 1 (Introductions)  
**Duration:** 5-7 minutes  
**Objective:** Name memorization; fun energizer  
**Materials:** Soft ball or crumpled paper  

**Instructions:**
1. Stand in circle
2. Toss ball to someone: "What's your name?"
3. That person responds: "My name is [name]"
4. They toss to someone else, who says their name
5. Then add: "I'm from [country]"
6. Then add: "I'm a [job]"

**Repeat 2 full rounds.**

## ICE BREAKER 6: Human Bingo

**Level:** Week 1-2  
**Duration:** 8-10 minutes  
**Objective:** Questions; circulation; socializing  
**Materials:** Bingo card (5x5 grid with characteristics)  

**Grid Example:**
```
Has blue eyes | Speaks French | Likes pizza | From Africa | Plays football
Has curly hair | Likes pizza | Can swim | Wears glasses | Likes music
Runs daily | Speaks 3+ languages | Likes pizza | Has siblings | From Europe
Plays sports | Wears red | Likes pizza | From Asia | Young (under 25)
Likes cooking | Has pets | Likes pizza | Likes reading | Center (FREE)
```

**Instructions:**
1. Learners walk around asking: "Do you have blue eyes?" "Are you from Africa?"
2. If yes, that person signs the box
3. First to get 5 in a row (horizontal, vertical, diagonal) wins

## ICE BREAKER 7: Emoji Feelings

**Level:** Week 1  
**Duration:** 3-4 minutes  
**Objective:** Mood check; vocabulary; quick energizer  
**Materials:** Printed emojis or draw on board  

**Instructions:**
1. Show emojis:
   - 😄 Happy
   - 😨 Scared
   - 🤔 Confused
   - 😮 Surprised
   - 😟 Worried
2. "What does this emoji mean in English?"
3. Class responds: "Happy!"
4. "Yes! Let's all look happy!" [Everyone makes face]
5. Repeat with 5-6 emojis

## ICE BREAKER 8: Quick Fire Countries

**Level:** Week 2 (Countries)  
**Duration:** 4-5 minutes  
**Objective:** Nationalities; pronunciation; speed  
**Materials:** None  

**Instructions:**
1. Teacher says a country: "France!"
2. First learner to say nationality wins point: "French!"
3. Teacher: "Japan!"
4. Next learner: "Japanese!"
5. Continue rapid-fire for 10-15 countries

**Points:**
- Correct & first: 1 point
- Correct but second: 0 points
- Incorrect: No point, next round

## ICE BREAKER 9: Stand Up If...

**Level:** Week 4 (Routine/Preferences)  
**Duration:** 3-5 minutes  
**Objective:** Energy boost; listening; humor  
**Materials:** None  

**Instructions:**
Teacher makes statements. Learners stand if TRUE for them.

Examples:
- "Stand up if you wake up before 7am!"
- "Stand up if you like coffee!"
- "Stand up if you have a cat!"
- "Stand up if you speak more than 2 languages!"
- "Stand up if you like Mondays!"

**Variation:** "Sit down if..." for variety.

---

## ICE BREAKER 10: Name + Action

**Level:** Week 1  
**Duration:** 5-7 minutes  
**Objective:** Name learning; kinesthetic activity; fun  
**Materials:** None  

**Instructions:**
1. Teacher goes first: "I'm [name] and I like... [performs action]" 
   - Example: "I'm Teacher and I like running!" [mime running]
2. Learner 1: "This is [Teacher] who likes running. I'm [Name] and I like dancing!" [mime dancing]
3. Learner 2: "This is [Teacher] who likes running, [Learner 1] who likes dancing. I'm [Name] and I like swimming!" [mime swimming]
4. Continue around class

**Note:** By end, see how many names/actions students remember!

---

## Ice Breaker Tips

✅ **Keep it SHORT:** Never more than 10 minutes  
✅ **Keep it SIMPLE:** Everyone can participate  
✅ **Keep it FUN:** Laughter = learning  
✅ **Rotate who goes first:** Vary participation patterns  
✅ **Use as energy boost:** When energy dips  
✅ **Mix with instruction:** Don't overdo ice breakers  
✅ **Celebrate participation:** Clap after each turn
